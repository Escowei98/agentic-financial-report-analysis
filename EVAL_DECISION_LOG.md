# Evaluation Decision Log

## [2026-03-23] Removed Corrupted GOOGL 2022 Document from Ablation Dataset
* **Context:** The Google 10-K document for FY 2022 (`10K_2023-02-03.txt`) was corrupted (only 1,980 characters parsed). Continuing the Optuna Ablation study across 80 questions included 3 questions referencing this broken document. Since evaluating on these questions leads to false and artificially lowered scores (score of 0), the results of our existing trials in `study.trials` were mathematically flawed. We wanted to preserve the 30 existing Optuna trials without rerunning the entire expensive evaluation.
* **Decision:** We extracted the 3 affected `GOOGL_2022` questions from `ablation_test_data.csv` so the next trials evaluate exactly 77 valid questions. Simultaneously, we updated the existing Optuna SQLite DB (`data/ablation_study.db`), applying a scaling factor of `80 / 77` (~`1.039`) to the existing scores (`composite_score`, `context_precision`, `context_recall`, `faithfulness`, `avg_latency`) under the mathematical assumption that these corrupted questions yielded 0 points.
* **Alternatives:** We rejected restarting the Optuna study from scratch (cost and time prohibitive), or merely dropping the document without mathematically scaling the historic Optuna trial values (which would have meant optimizing against an inconsistent baseline where earlier trials are at an artificial disadvantage).
